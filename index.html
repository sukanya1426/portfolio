About Me

I am a final-year undergraduate student in Software Engineering at the Institute of Information Technology (IIT), University of Dhaka.

My research explores how we can build AI systems that are reliable, verifiable, and useful in real-world settings. At the UIUC++ Summer Research Program, I worked with Prof. Tianyin Xu on the TLAPS Proof Benchmark, evaluating whether AI models can synthesize mechanically verified, formally correct TLA+ proofs. I have also worked as a Research Assistant with Dr. Md. Shariful Islam on the UGC-funded TestCube project, where I worked on AI-assisted Android testing and automated UI exploration and verification.

Previously, I worked with the Computational Intelligence and Operations Laboratory (CIOL) on research examining human–AI interaction in low-tech, low-resource healthcare systems, with a focus on how language, trust, infrastructure, and human oversight shape the use of AI in these settings.

Working across formal verification, software testing, and human–AI interaction has given me the opportunity to approach AI research from different domains and think about how similar reliability and interaction challenges appear in different settings.

Research Interests: HCI, AI–Computer Interaction, Reliable Autonomous Agents, Formal Methods & Verification, AI for Software Engineering, Model Optimization

I am actively preparing for graduate studies ( PhD ) in Software Engineering.
